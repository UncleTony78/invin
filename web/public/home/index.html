Questions before your first invoice

Who is InVin for?

Creators, freelancers and small studios who bill clients and get paid by bank transfer.

Which currencies can I bill in?

US dollars, British pounds, euros and Nigerian naira. Each customer keeps their own currency.

Do my customers need an account?

No. They open the invoice from your email, see your bank details and download the PDF.

Do I have to chase late payments?

No. Choose when reminders go in Settings. They stop as soon as the invoice is paid.

Can I send estimates first?

Yes. Customers accept online, and an accepted estimate becomes an invoice in one click.

How do I sign in?

With your email and a 6-digit code. There's no password to remember.

Send your first invoice today

Sign in with your email and a 6-digit code. Setting up takes about a minute.
