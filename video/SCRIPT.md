# InVin launch video: script, hooks and sound

Voice: warm, unhurried, a little dry. One narrator throughout. Music sits under the voice and swells on the end card.

## Launch film (16:9, about 45 s)

| Time | Picture | Voiceover | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Hook.** B-roll: a freelancer at a laptop late, then a phone face-down on the desk. Type over it: "Invoice sent: 21 days ago." | "You finished the work three weeks ago. You're still waiting to get paid." | Room tone, a clock tick, music starts low |
| 0:05 | The InVin mark builds, the slot cuts in | "Meet InVin." | Soft whoosh into the logo, a low "thock" as the slot cuts |
| 0:07 | "Invoicing for freelancers and small studios." | "Invoicing for freelancers and small studios." | Music lifts |
| 0:11 | An invoice fills in, line by line; the total counts up | "Make a branded invoice in a minute. Your customer's currency and terms fill in for you." | Light key ticks on each line |
| 0:18 | Sent. Opened. Paid. The PAID stamp lands | "Send it, see the moment it's opened, and get paid by bank transfer." | A soft send whoosh, a notification chime, a stamp thump on "paid" |
| 0:24 | B-roll: a client on a phone, tapping to pay | (music only) | Phone tap |
| 0:26 | Nine designs fan in and change colour | "Pick from nine designs, in your colours." | Paper slides, one per design |
| 0:31 | Notifications stack: reminder, opened, payment, receipt | "InVin sends the polite reminders, and the receipt the minute you're paid." | Small pops as each one lands |
| 0:38 | B-roll: a small studio at work. Then 9 · 4 · 5 · 60 | "Four currencies. Your whole team. Your own email domain." | Rolling ticks on the numbers |
| 0:43 | End card: InVin, "Send your first invoice today.", Create your account | "InVin. Send your first invoice today." | Music resolves, one clean button click |

## Vertical cuts (9:16, 15 to 20 s each), three hooks

Each opens on its hook in the first second, then shows the product doing exactly that, then the end card.

**A. "Stop chasing invoices."**
- 0:00 Big type on b-roll of someone checking their banking app: "Stop chasing invoices." VO: "Stop chasing invoices."
- 0:02 Reminder notifications stack. VO: "InVin sends polite reminders before and after the due date..."
- 0:08 PAID stamp. VO: "...and stops the moment you're paid."
- 0:12 End card. VO: "InVin. Invoicing for freelancers."

**B. "Your invoice could look like this."**
- 0:00 One design snaps to the next on every beat. VO: "Your invoice could look like this. Or this. Or this."
- 0:06 Colours sweep across the nine designs. VO: "Nine designs, your colours, one click."
- 0:11 End card. VO: "InVin. Send your first invoice today."

**C. "Know the second they open it."**
- 0:00 Phone with the "Opened" notification. VO: "Know the second your client opens your invoice."
- 0:03 Sent, Opened, Paid. VO: "Sent. Opened. Paid. And the receipt goes out by itself."
- 0:10 End card. VO: "InVin. Send your first invoice today."

## Sound

- **Music:** warm minimal electronic, about 100 BPM, soft piano and a muted pluck. It starts sparse under the hook, lifts at the headline, and resolves on the end card. It ducks under the voice.
- **Effects:** a soft whoosh on each transition, light key ticks as lines fill in, a send whoosh, a notification chime, a stamp thump, small notification pops, a final button click. Short, quiet and dry, never cartoonish.
